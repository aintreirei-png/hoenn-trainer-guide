    Hoenn Routes

    These five places are where most trainers begin. You usually visit them in this order:

    1. Littleroot Town: your home town and where the adventure starts.
    2. Route 101: a short path where you get your starter Pokémon.
    3. Oldale Town: the first town with a Poké Mart for supplies.
    4. Route 102: a good place to catch your first wild Pokémon and train.
    5. Rustboro City: a larger city with the first Gym.

    Game Tips

    Choosing Your Hoenn Starter

    All three starters can finish the game. Pick the one you like best. Mudkip and Treecko have an easier time at the first Gym.

    Read about starters

    Finding Pokémon on Route 102

    Walk in the tall grass to meet wild Pokémon. Catch a few different ones so your team is not only one type.

    Route 102 details

    Preparing for the Rustboro Gym

    The leader uses Rock-type Pokémon. Level up your team, buy Potions, and bring a Water or Grass Pokémon if you can.
